# Lois_Synthesis
## Problem Statement

In Ghana, readers may find it difficult to discover books that match their interests, age groups, and reading levels, while also finding books that reflect Ghanaian culture, history, and everyday experiences.
**The Reading Room** addresses this problem by providing a simple, user-friendly website where readers can explore books by genre and audience, search for titles, save their favourite books, and receive personalized recommendations through a short quiz. It also highlights Ghanaian literature to encourage readers to discover local authors, stories, and cultural heritage.
